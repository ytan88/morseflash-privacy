MorseFlash Support

Need help? You're in the right place.

MorseFlash lets you send and receive Morse code using your iPhone's flashlight and camera. Below are quick instructions, troubleshooting tips, and how to reach us.

How to Use

Transmit (send a signal):

  • Open the TRANSMIT tab.
  • Type your message.
  • Start transmission — MorseFlash blinks the message in Morse code using your flashlight (torch).
  • Use the loop option to repeat the message continuously.

Receive (decode a signal):

  • Open the RECEIVE tab.
  • Point your camera at a flashing light source.
  • Hold the device steady and centered on the light — MorseFlash decodes the flashes into text in real time.
  • For a light that is far away, use the zoom buttons or pinch the camera view.

Record a video (version 1.1 and later, optional):

  • Turn on RECORD VIDEO, then start receiving.
  • When the app says a loop is captured, tap STOP & SAVE, or keep recording for more loops. The video is saved to Photos when you stop.

AI decode (version 1.1 and later, optional):

  • On the RECEIVE tab, tap AI, connect an API key from your own Anthropic Console account, and turn AI decode on.
  • Record a transmission, then tap STOP & SEND TO CLAUDE in the reminder (or DECODE WITH CLAUDE) and keep the app open. Each decode is billed to your Anthropic account.

Permissions

  • Camera — used to receive, and needed by iOS to control the flashlight. Camera pictures are saved only with RECORD VIDEO and uploaded only when you use AI decode.
  • Photos (add only) — used by RECORD VIDEO to save your videos.
  • Flashlight — used by the Transmit feature to blink your message.

If you previously denied a permission, enable it in Settings → MorseFlash and allow Camera or Photos access.

Troubleshooting

  • Flashlight doesn't blink: Make sure the device isn't in Low Power Mode and the flashlight isn't already in use by another app or Control Center. The torch may be temporarily disabled if the device is too hot.
  • Receiver isn't decoding: Hold the phone steady, keep the light source centered in the preview, and reduce background light or glare. Very fast or irregular flashing may not decode reliably.
  • A recording didn't reach Photos: Allow MorseFlash to add to Photos in Settings → MorseFlash, then open the app's RECEIVE tab. The app keeps the recording and saves it then.
  • Camera preview is black: Confirm Camera permission is granted in Settings → MorseFlash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does MorseFlash need an internet connection?
No. Sending and receiving work fully offline. Only the optional AI decode needs the internet. (Version 1.0 also uses it to show ads.)

Do you collect my data?
We have no servers and don't receive your data. If you use AI decode, pictures from your recording go to Anthropic with your own API key. Version 1.0 shows ads through Google AdMob. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is MorseFlash free?
Yes. Version 1.1 and later has no ads. AI decode is optional, and Anthropic bills you for what you use.
