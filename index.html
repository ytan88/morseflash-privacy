Privacy Policy for MorseFlash

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MorseFlash (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use it. MorseFlash is a free utility that transmits Morse code using your device’s flashlight (torch) and receives Morse light signals using your device’s camera.

Information We Collect

We do not require an account and do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contacts.

Camera. When you use the receive feature, the app accesses your camera to detect light signals in real time. Camera frames are processed on your device only. We do not store, save, upload, or transmit any images or video.

Flashlight (torch). The transmit feature controls your device’s flashlight. This involves no data collection.

Advertising data (via Google AdMob). The app displays banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier / IDFA if you grant permission), IP address, general device information, and ad interaction data. This collection is performed by Google, not by us. See Google’s policies linked below.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS may ask whether you allow the app to track you across apps and websites. If you allow tracking, AdMob may show personalized ads. If you decline, AdMob still shows ads, but they are non-personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by our advertising partner is used to display ads, measure ad performance, and support the continued development of this free app.

Third-Party Services

MorseFlash uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management).

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising data is processed by Google in accordance with its policies.

Children’s Privacy

MorseFlash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app’s advertising, please contact us so it can be addressed.

Data Retention

We do not retain personal information on our own servers. Any data collected for advertising is retained by Google according to its retention policies.

Your Choices

  • Decline tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Reset or limit your Advertising Identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.
  • Stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
