Privacy Policy for MorseFlash

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MorseFlash (“the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information. MorseFlash sends Morse code with your iPhone’s flashlight and reads Morse light signals with its camera.

This policy describes version 1.1 and later. Version 1.0 is different: see Version 1.0 below.

In short

  • No ads, no tracking, and no MorseFlash account.
  • We have no servers. We don’t receive your messages, pictures, videos or API key.
  • Sending and reading signals happens on your iPhone.
  • If you set up the optional AI decode and choose to use it, pictures from your recording go to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude.

Camera and flashlight

The app uses the camera to read light signals and to control the flashlight. Camera pictures are processed on your iPhone. They are saved only if you turn on RECORD VIDEO, and sent only if you use AI decode.

Recordings (optional)

RECORD VIDEO is off until you turn it on. It saves video, without sound, to your Photos library. The app can add to Photos but can’t see what is already there. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs these videos like your other videos.

  • The app keeps a working copy until the video is saved to Photos. If saving fails, it keeps that copy and tries again.
  • While AI decode is set up, the app also keeps a copy of your latest recording. It removes that copy when you start the next recording or turn AI decode off, after any save or decode that is using it has ended.
  • These copies are not included in device backups, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app never deletes videos from Photos. You can delete them there.

AI decode with Claude (optional)

AI decode is off until you connect your own Anthropic API key and turn it on.

What is sent to Anthropic:

  • When you tap CONNECT: your API key and a very small test request, to check the key.
  • When you tap DECODE WITH CLAUDE or STOP & SEND TO CLAUDE: black-and-white still pictures from your recording (one transmission, or up to its last 3 minutes), their times, and a short technical description. The pictures can show people and places near the light.
  • With every request, as with any internet service: your IP address and your API key, which identifies your Anthropic account.

How long Anthropic keeps it:

  • The app deletes the uploaded files when a decode ends. If it can’t, they expire after one hour. Anthropic may keep expired files for a limited time for safety review.
  • Anthropic normally deletes API requests and answers within 30 days, and keeps data from the analysis for up to 30 days.
  • If its safety systems flag a request, Anthropic may keep it for up to 2 years and the safety scores for up to 7 years. It may also keep data where the law requires or where your agreement with Anthropic says so.

Your use of the Claude API is under your agreement with Anthropic. See Anthropic’s Privacy Policy, Commercial Terms and data retention rules.

What else is stored on your iPhone

  • Your API key, in the iPhone Keychain, on that phone only. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so tap DISCONNECT before deleting the app if you want the key removed. If you install MorseFlash again, it removes a key left behind.
  • A few settings, such as RECORD VIDEO and AI decode on or off.
  • A diagnostic log, without message text. We don’t receive it. It can be part of your device backup.
  • Received messages are kept only in memory, until the app closes.

Sharing and sale

We don’t sell personal information. The app shares data only with Anthropic, and only when you use AI decode as described above.

Children’s Privacy

MorseFlash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has given us information.

Your choices

  • Leave RECORD VIDEO and AI decode off. Everything else works without them.
  • Turn AI decode off or tap DISCONNECT at any time. A decode that is running stops, and nothing more from the recording is sent. What was already sent follows the retention rules above.
  • Change camera and Photos access in Settings → MorseFlash.

Version 1.0

Version 1.0 has no recording and no AI decode, and does not save or upload camera pictures. It shows banner ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, device information and ad interaction data, and uses the Google User Messaging Platform for consent. Google handles this data under its Privacy Policy and its rules for apps that use its services. You can change tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or update to version 1.1 or later, which has no ads.
